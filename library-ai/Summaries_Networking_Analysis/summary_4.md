# Lecture Summary on Network Concepts

## Key Topics

### 1. Network Applications
* **Power Plants**: Nodes represent power plants; edges symbolize connections between them.
* **Phone Networks**: Phones are nodes, with edges representing communication lines.
* **Stock Market**: Stocks as nodes; edges are inferred based on correlation, not explicitly observed.
* **Sports**: Teams as nodes, with edges representing games played between them.
* **Biological Networks**: Proteins as nodes; edges depict protein interactions or complexes.
* **Family Trees**: Individuals as nodes; edges indicate familial connections (e.g., parent-child relationships).
* **Recommendation Systems**: In systems like Airbnb, tenants and properties form nodes, with edges based on interactions and ratings.

### 2. Network Language
* **Simple Network**: Basic structure with nodes and edges; no self-loops or directed edges.
* **Self Loop**: A node connected to itself; not permissible in simple networks.
* **Multigraphs**: Networks allowing multiple edges between two nodes (e.g., roads between cities, synapses between neurons).

## Summary
Various applications of networks were discussed, including power plants, phone networks, and the stock market, highlighting nodes and edges.

## Tools
* **Network Visualization Tools**: Used for illustrating connections among nodes and edges in various networks.
* **Statistical Correlation Methods**: Helps infer relationships in networks like the stock market.

## ONE-SENTENCE TAKEAWAY
Understanding diverse network applications enhances our comprehension of relationships and interactions across different fields.
