# Lecture Summary on Network Science and Criminal Networks

## SUMMARY
This lecture discusses weighted degrees, centrality measures, and the application of network science in understanding criminal networks.

---

## Topics Discussed

### Weighted Degree
- **Definition**: The sum of weights of all edges connected to a node, providing a measure of its importance in the context of the network.

### Eigenvector Centrality
- **Definition**: A measure that indicates the influence of a node in a network, taking into account the importance of its neighboring nodes.

### Closeness Centrality
- **Definition**: A measure of how close a node is to all other nodes in the network, calculated by considering the weights of edges.

### Betweeness Centrality
- **Definition**: A measure of a node's role in connecting other nodes, indicating how often it lies on the shortest path between two other nodes.

### Application of Network Science in Criminal Networks
- **Definition**: The use of network analysis to understand the structure and dynamics of criminal networks, particularly their responses to law enforcement actions.

#### Case Study: Calviore Drug Network
- **Overview**: A detailed examination of a Montreal-based drug network and its evolution during police investigations.
  - Initial focus on specific individuals, like Larry, and how the network adapts to ongoing police actions.
  - Examination of geographic shifts in drug importation routes, showcasing the network's resilience and adaptive strategies.
