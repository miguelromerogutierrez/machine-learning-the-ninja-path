# Lecture Summary on Network Analysis

## Overview of Connected Components
- **Connected Component**: A subset of a graph where there is a path between any two vertices.
  - **Large Component**: Contains a substantial majority, potentially 99.9% of the nodes, often containing key users.
  - **Small Components**: Include ghost accounts or isolated groups (e.g., school-specific networks).

## Identifying and Analyzing Key Nodes
- **Degree**: The number of edges connected to a node, indicative of a node's level of connectivity.
  - **Celebrities or Influencers**: Typically nodes with a high degree, meaning they are connected to many others.
  
## Degree Distribution
- **Degree Distribution Plot**:
  - X-axis: Degree (number of connections).
  - Y-axis: Proportion of nodes with that degree.
  - Example Data:
    - Degree 0: ~33% of nodes.
    - Degree 1: ~45% of nodes.
    - Degree 2: ~10% of nodes.
    
## Log-Log Transformation
- **Log-Log Plot**: A transformed visualization using logarithmic scales on both axes.
  - Purpose: To better visualize nodes with very high degrees that were previously obscured by scale limitations.

## Summary
> The lecture covers the significance of connected components, node degrees, and the importance of degree distribution in network analysis.

## Tools
